Psychology

Psychology is the scientific study of behavior, thoughts, and emotions. It’s a great major for students interested in understanding people and improving mental health and well-being.

What You'll Learn:

  • Human development and cognition
  • Research methods and data analysis
  • Abnormal and social psychology
  • Therapeutic approaches

Career Paths:

  • Counselor or therapist
  • Social worker
  • Human resources specialist
  • Academic researcher
